## Editorial recommendations
1. Write genuinely useful introductions and include clear prep/cook times, servings, ingredients, method, substitutions, storage notes, and safety guidance where relevant.
2. Add original step photos over time. Only mark up visible, accurate information in Recipe structured data.
3. Avoid keyword stuffing and mass-producing near-identical pages. One page should satisfy one clear search intent.
4. Keep seasonal articles current when the advice genuinely changes; don't change dates just to look fresh.
5. After the domain is live, verify Google Search Console, submit the sitemap, inspect key URLs, and use query/page reports to update content.
6. Before monetization applications, ensure About, Contact, Privacy, and Disclaimer pages accurately reflect the actual business, data practices, and advertising partners.
